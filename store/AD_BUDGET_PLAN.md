## Gate: do not spend anything until all of these are true
1. The game has been played by 5–10 testers and the fixes are in (see `NEXT_STEPS.md` 6).
2. It is published on Google Play, at least as an open test or a soft launch.
3. Crash-free sessions are at or above 99.5 % and the ANR rate is low (Play Console → Android vitals).
4. Analytics are integrated with consent, so installs, D1/D7 retention and session length can be measured.
5. Real gameplay has been captured: 3–5 short videos of 15–30 s each, portrait, showing Arc Storm, a boss telegraph and a build coming together. **Never use footage of anything that is not in the game.**

**Controls**
* Set a **daily budget cap** of about $20–40 per day in phase 1, and **stop the campaign** at each phase's limit.
* Review weekly. Use one spreadsheet with spend, installs, CPI, D1, D7 and notes.
* Pause immediately after any crash spike or bad review wave.
* Keep store-listing experiments (icon, first screenshots) running for free alongside.

**What the numbers mean**
* CPI is the cost per install.
* D1/D7 retention is the share of new players who come back after 1 and 7 days.
* With no ads or purchases in the game, this spend buys **players and data, not revenue**. Decide whether that is the goal before approving it.

**Approval needed from you:** the countries, the maximum CPI, the daily caps, and whether to run phases 3–4 at all.
