## What this game collects
Evil Cats in its current version **does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.**

* **Progress** (missions, currencies, settings, the display name you type) is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage.
* The game has **no advertising, analytics, account sign-in, cloud save or online features** in this version, and needs no internet permission.
* The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings → Reset progress** in the game, or uninstall the game. Both delete all saved progress from your device.

## Children
The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
